Switching math neurons off

Each neuron is held at its average value on non-math text, at every token. One at a time, nothing happens to GSM8K. Switched off together, the top 5, 10, 20 or 50 cost 2.6–4.6 points, below every random set drawn from the same layers, while arithmetic stays put. Perplexity on GSM8K-style text jumps because Base has memorized those questions.
